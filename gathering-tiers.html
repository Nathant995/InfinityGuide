Mining: which pickaxe?

PickaxeNew materials at this tier
StoneStone, Rune Essence, Clay
BoneCopper, Tin, Sandstone
BronzeIron, Silver, Granite
IronGold, Coal, Dragon Tooth; use Iron for Blurite (source tables disagree)
SteelMithril
MithrilAdamantite, Limestone, Gypsum
AdamantRunite
RuneHighest-tier mining; check the current node prompt.

Higher tiers retain access to earlier materials. If a node refuses your tool, read its requirement instead of repeatedly swinging. The table follows the supplied Mining snapshot; newly added resources may have separate requirements.

Woodcutting: which axe?

Logging axeAdditional trees at this tier
StoneAsh
BronzeOak
IronWillow, Blightwood, Bloodwood
SteelDead trees, Thorny Irit
MithrilMaple
AdamantYew
RuneMagic

Use normal swings on a standing tree and overhead chops on fallen timber. Position so falling trees and enemies do not trap you. Preserve stumps if you want trees to regrow; the saved pages disagree on exact real-world respawn timing, so this guide does not promise a timer.

Skill spells that change gathering

UnlockPractical use
Mining 11 · RocksplosionBreak nearby rock within the spell’s area. Useful for clustered deposits.
Mining 24 · Stone SpiritsHelp locate a nearby clay or ore deposit; a direction hint still needs a safe route.
Mining 80 · Divine RockUses five ore to transform a node. Read the current spell costs before casting.
Woodcutting 11 · Axtral ProjectionA magical aid for cutting trees. Bring the required runes.
Woodcutting 27 · SplinterHelps process fallen logs.
Woodcutting 50 / 52 · Trunk Totem*A later gathering aid; consult the current spell description.
Field note

Level unlocks give you options, not free casts. Carry the required runes and consider whether a spell saves enough time to justify its materials.
