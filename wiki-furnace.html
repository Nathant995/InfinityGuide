Using this station

Unlock by acquiring copper or tin. Load ore into the input slots and Charcoal into fuel. Bronze uses both ores in one recipe: 3 Copper Ore + 3 Tin Ore produce 2 Bronze Bars. A Furnace does not fire clay.
