Skill referencesMining · XP & unlocks

Mine copper and tin with a Bone Pickaxe; return to smelt both into Bronze Bars. A Mining level does not replace the required pickaxe tier. Keep charcoal production running so the ore does not sit unused.

Skill referencesWoodcutting · XP & unlocks

Fell a tree with the normal attack, then use Overhead Chop on the fallen trunk after unlocking it at level 4. Save Ash Logs for charcoal and early benches; save Oak Logs for bronze tools. Overhead Chop does little damage to standing trees.

Skill referencesFishing · XP & unlocks

Make a Coarse Net from 2 Coarse Thread at a Crafting Table. Net fish until Fishing 3, then craft an Ash Rod from 1 Ash Log + 1 Coarse Thread. Net and rod spots are separate. Bait reduces junk; pulling against the fish tires it before reeling.

Skill referencesAgility · XP & unlocks

Walk between gathering areas to gain exploration XP, or run the courses unlocked for your region. Start with Brynmoor courses. Double Jump unlocks at 10; Air Dash at 32. Do not plan a gap around an ability you have not unlocked.

Skill referencesMagic · XP & unlocks

Craft a staff or wand, equip it, then select a compatible combat rune in the inventory. Air is available first, Water unlocks at 7 and Fire at 21. Combat casts consume runes; utility spells have their own skill unlocks and costs.

Skill referencesConstruction · XP & unlocks

Build the objects your camp needs rather than filling the area with disposable pieces. Medium pieces unlock at 3, small pieces at 5 and triangular pieces at 8. Construction 11 unlocks Eye of Oculus for an easier building viewpoint.

Skill referencesArtisan · XP & unlocks

Process materials you already gather: flax into thread, logs into charcoal, and ore into bars. The recipe database records XP per batch, so compare output quantity before assuming the displayed XP is per item. Sawmill recipes in this snapshot award 0 XP.
