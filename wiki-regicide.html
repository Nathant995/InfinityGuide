Regicide

Prepare for the Scorned Wild and the conclusion of the Kuldra Saga.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 12 OCT 2026
On this page
  • Reach Rasthin’s Rest
  • Prepare as a group
  • Finish the saga

Reach Rasthin’s Rest

  1. Complete Icthlarin’s Little Helper. Travel to the Dragon Gate north-west of Velgar’s Rise in the Fractured Plains.
  2. Enter the Scorned Wild to the north and find Zilyana at Rasthin’s Rest.
  3. Use the permitted hub area to prepare. Coordinate equipment, supplies and roles before starting the raid at Dragontail Vines.

Your checklist

Field note

The supplied Regicide page describes the route and rewards but does not document every raid mechanic. Read the Scorned Wild and Kuldra wiki guides before committing to the encounter; do not treat this checklist as a full raid strategy.

Finish the saga

Complete the raid and defeat Kuldra, then return to Zilyana. Listed rewards include the Dragon Kiteshield, Dragonslayer Cape, Queenslayer Pack and Lougrim’s Staff. For the optional follow-up, take the staff to Ancamairon at the shrine.

Completing the saga gives the shared adventure a milestone. Building, skilling, gathering and helping the next new arrival remain worthwhile goals afterwards.
