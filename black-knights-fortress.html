Black Knight’s Fortress

A practical checklist for Dowdun’s 13 Dreaming Stone shards.

3 MIN READREVIEWED 12 OCT 2026
On this page
  • Open the way
  • Pack before leaving
  • The 13-shard checklist
  • Restore the stone and face the Titan

Open the way

  1. After Withering Heights, speak to Death in the Forgotten Temple. Meet Cathan at the ominous gate south of Silverthorn Keep, east of Fellhollow.
  2. Travel through the Approach into the Courtyard and speak to the Wise Old Man at the base.
  3. Go to the Nexus and speak to Doric. Inspect the Dreaming Stone: you need 13 shards. Activate nodestones as you explore to make return trips easier.

Pack before leaving

  • A Steel or Undead Spade, a suitable pickaxe (Steel or better is a useful expedition choice) and a Mithril Logging Axe.
  • A Fishing Rod, five Maple Sap and Air Runes for Windstep.
  • Food, healing, repaired equipment and spare inventory space.
Field note

Old walkthroughs sometimes rely on Feather Fall equipment to survive drops. Do not assume those older effects still apply. Use a safe route and your current spell descriptions.

Restore the stone and face the Titan

  1. Return all 13 shards to the Dreaming Stone and complete Doric’s dialogue. Choose the destruction option when asked to remove the barrier. Read the actual dialogue before selecting.
  2. Enter the Bastion. Use Windstep for the broken stair gaps and prepare for the Titan encounter.
  3. After defeating the Titan, return to the stone. The route into the Umbral Sands opens.

If another barrier blocks a route, check nearby nodestones and unresolved objectives. The Bastion barrier is tied to completing the shard objective.
