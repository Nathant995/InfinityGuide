Woodcutting · XP & unlocks

Fell a tree with the normal attack, then use Overhead Chop on the fallen trunk after unlocking it at level 4. Save Ash Logs for charcoal and early benches; save Oak Logs for bronze tools. Overhead Chop does little damage to standing trees.

How to train it

Fell a tree with the normal attack, then use Overhead Chop on the fallen trunk after unlocking it at level 4. Save Ash Logs for charcoal and early benches; save Oak Logs for bronze tools. Overhead Chop does little damage to standing trees.

Conflicting unlock levels in the source

The saved page lists Trunk Totem at 50 in its unlock table and 52 in its spell summary; Axtral Projection’s upgrade is listed at 78 and 88. These entries are marked with *. Check the milestone in your skill screen before planning for either level. The guide does not silently choose one.
