Ashenfall: a short field history

The people, powers and old conflicts behind the place you are exploring.

A fresh start on an old continent

Your adventurer comes to Ashenfall seeking a fresh start. The continent is saturated with anima, the magical energy behind its unusual life and powerful abilities. Ruins are more than scenery: they connect your survival journey to the history of RuneScape’s world.

Dragonwilds uses the RuneScape setting and familiar characters, while telling a survival adventure on Ashenfall. Shared names do not mean every Old School RuneScape storyline happened in exactly the same way.

The powers you will hear about

NameA useful first association
GuthixBalance; his protection and the continent’s isolation matter to its history.

These are starting associations, not a complete account of each god or follower. The linked Gods article explores their histories in more detail.

The older story — optional spoilers

Why Ashenfall’s past still matters

The supplied histories describe Guthix shielding persecuted people changed by anima, the Amalgamated. Dragonkin later arrived and left experiments and vaults across the continent. Conflicts involving humans, goblins and the Garou reshaped its settlements.

Those old struggles remain visible in the places you explore. Velgar’s blood and poison connect to the blighted landscape; Dragonkin artefacts and the threat of Kuldra draw your expedition into a much larger conflict.

Your place in the story

The Kuldra Saga takes you from basic survival to helping communities and confronting dragons. Each new region asks you to improve tools, supplies and knowledge. The practical loop—gather, craft, explore, return—supports the narrative rather than sitting apart from it.
