Keeping yourself safe

Protect your character, your supplies and the people playing alongside you.

Before leaving camp

Your checklist

Saved on this browser only. Tick items as you go.

Health, supplies and hazards

Keep food accessible and read the current item effects. Do not rely on old guides that describe a different health system. The 1.0 release notes describe a healing rework and the removal of Wardstone; your current health display and item descriptions are the useful reference.

  • Poison: watch the status indicator. Leave the affected area and use the appropriate remedy if available. Plan protection before entering known toxic terrain.
  • Falling: look for a walkable descent. Do not assume a movement spell is available or will save you.
  • Water and terrain: check the landing and escape route before jumping into an unfamiliar area.
  • Overconfidence: a stronger teammate surviving a region does not mean a new character is ready for it.

If you die

  1. Stop and read the game’s recovery information. Death consequences depend on world settings.
  2. Check the map and identify where you died.
  3. Prepare a safe return trip or ask the group for help.
  4. Recover only when you can deal with the threat that caused the death.
  5. Afterwards, repair, restock and decide what needs to change before another attempt.
Field note

Do not assume DragonInfinity has a particular death penalty, invulnerability setting or automatic item recovery. Ask the host about the current custom settings.

A shared world needs care

The server screenshot shows PvP enabled. Avoid swinging or testing spells next to players. Consent and the host’s rules matter even when the game permits damage. Keep personal chests and unfinished builds intact.

A multiplayer menu may leave the world running. Move somewhere safe before reading a long guide or stepping away; when in doubt, leave the server rather than assuming you have paused it.

Keep accounts safe too

Share the server password only through the host’s agreed channels. Never give someone your platform account password, two-factor code or recovery code. Use official game updates and ask the host before installing anything claimed to be required for joining.
