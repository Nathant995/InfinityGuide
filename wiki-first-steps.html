Requirements, unlocks & recipes

How this works

First Steps is a primary quest that teaches you the basics of the game before introducing you to the narrative. It is recommended for first-time players to not skip through these quests and jump into Dragon Slayer so that they can fully understand the mechanics of the game. Go and speak to the Wise Old Man in the temple. He will tell you about the dragon and offer to teach you about the basics, which you can either accept or refuse. If you refuse it, you can talk with him again in Bramblemead Village to finish the quest. If you accept the tutorial, he will send you to Doric just outside the temple, starting Getting Started .

What to do

  1. Speak to the Wise Old Man at the temple and accept his introduction.
  2. Follow the introductory activities to meet Doric and Vannaka.
  3. Use the quests and Journal to complete the gathering, crafting and combat lessons.
  4. Meet the Wise Old Man in Bramblemead Village to continue the main story.
