Keybinds & controls

A compact reference for keyboard, mouse and Xbox-style controller prompts.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 09 OCT 2026

  • Movement and interaction
  • Menus and quick access
  • Build, inspect, then place

Find your actual bindings

Open Esc → Settings → Controls. Rebound keys and platform layouts can differ. The reference below combines Jagex’s keyboard guide with the 1.0 controller changes and the supplied screenshots. The on-screen action prompt always takes priority.

Controller change

In 1.0, primary attack moved to RB/R1/R and special attack to RT/R2/ZR. Some older guides still show the reverse.

Build, inspect, then place

In Build mode, keyboard players can use Q/E for categories, Y for free placement, T for repair and Esc to cancel. Always read the active mode: an input used to attack in the world may place or remove something while building.

Make the controls comfortable

  • Practise dodge and block before entering a tough fight.
  • If two actions conflict after rebinding, review both affected menus.
  • Keep the camera sensitivity low enough that you can turn without losing track of your target.
  • Try a short gathering trip after changing your controls; it is easier to discover a bad binding there than during a boss fight.
