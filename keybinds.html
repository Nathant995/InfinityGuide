Keybinds & controls

Platform controls and the steps for tracking quests, crafting, equipping and building.

3 MIN READREVIEWED 12 OCT 2026

Choose your platform

PC means keyboard and mouse. A controller connected to a PC uses its controller layout. Console coverage is Xbox Series X|S, PS5 and Nintendo Switch 2.

Source confidence

Attack and special attack are confirmed for all three controllers by Jagex’s 1.0 notes. PS5’s remaining layout is documented by the linked current controller guide. Entries marked * are the corresponding Xbox/Switch physical-button mappings, not independently verified native screenshots. Check Settings → Controls before relying on those mappings.

Open menus and follow a quest

  1. Open the map/journal: M / J. On controllers, use the tabs shown across the top to reach the quest list.
  2. Select the quest name, then choose its track/select prompt. Read the current objective before leaving the menu.
  3. Return to the world using the displayed Back prompt. Face the marker on the compass and open the map again when the terrain blocks a direct route.
  4. At the quest giver or object, use Interact E. Select the dialogue answer you want rather than repeatedly pressing Continue.

Craft an item from a walkthrough

  1. Stand close to the workstation and use Interact E.
  2. Select its recipe category using the displayed tab controls. Highlight the item and compare your held ingredients with the required quantities.
  3. Choose the shown Craft/Start prompt. Processing stations may also need fuel; collect the result from the output slot.
  4. Open Inventory Tab. Highlight the item and use its equip or quick-access assignment prompt. Use Quick access 1–8 to bring it into your hand.

Build and repair

Open Build Mode B. Choose a category, select a piece, rotate the preview and confirm when placement is valid. PC category keys are Q/E, free placement is Y and repair is T. Controller placement, rotation, repair and removal use context-specific prompts; do not reuse a combat button without reading the build HUD.

Field note

Destroy, drop and dismantle actions are not interchangeable. Read the selected object name before holding any removal action.

Change a binding

From the main menu, choose Settings → Controls. In a world, pause with Esc, then open Settings → Controls. Select the action and use the available rebind prompt. If your platform does not offer a rebind option, use its displayed default layout.

Sources: Jagex keyboard/control reference; Jagex 1.0 changes; current PS5 controller reference. Jagex’s older help table lists conflicting inventory and attack bindings; this guide does not use those older controller entries.
