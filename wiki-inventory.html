Inventory, weight & equipment

Understand what you are carrying and what is actually helping you.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 12 OCT 2026

A slot is not the same as weight

Your bag can become heavy before it runs out of spaces. The standard starting weight limit in the supplied snapshot is 400. Equipment, perks and server settings can change your situation; read the current displayed limit.

Over the limit, you become encumbered: running and jumping are unavailable, movement slows and automatic pickup stops. Leave ore or timber in appropriate storage before setting off again.

Use the right inventory section

  • Runes, ammunition and quest items have their own inventory sections. Check those tabs before deciding an item is missing.
  • Putting a weapon in your bag does not equip it. Assign it to the action bar and select it.
  • Compare equipment power, durability and useful effects. A stronger item left broken or unequipped will not help you.
  • Keep at least one spare slot for quest rewards and important boss drops.
