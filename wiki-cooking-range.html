Requirements, unlocks & recipes

How this works

The cooking range can be used to create more complex dishes than can be made on the campfire or grill . It functions like the stone range , but can craft a wider variety of recipes when unlocked at level 16 Cooking . It requires a roof above it to function. NOTE: To learn the recipe (see it in the build menu), you MUST interact with the basic campfire at a certain cooking level, on top of having obtained all the materials required to build it.

Use these tables to check the exact requirements in your supplied Dragonwilds wiki snapshot. Search for an item, ingredient, station or level. Recipes may change with game updates.

What to do

  1. Find or build this station at an accessible crafting area.
  2. Inspect the recipe you want to produce and identify missing materials.
  3. Gather or process its inputs at any prerequisite stations.
  4. Use the station interface to craft your item and collect the output.
  5. Store excess materials in the correctly labelled communal chest.
