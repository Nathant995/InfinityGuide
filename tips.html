Survive & thrive

Hints, tips & tricks

Small habits that make the next session easier.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 09 OCT 2026
On this page
  • Ten habits worth learning
  • Do not copy a veteran’s loadout blindly
  • When a guide seems wrong
  • A useful end-of-session routine

Ten habits worth learning

  1. Plan one useful task. “Make a better tool” is easier to finish than “collect everything.”
  2. Read names, not just icons. Similar-looking materials may have different uses.
  3. Check what is equipped. Your best weapon does nothing while sitting unused in your bag.
  4. Repair on arrival. Make it part of your camp routine so the next trip starts ready.
  5. Sort before crafting. It is easier to understand shortages when your supplies are organised.
  6. Visit the skill menu after a level. You may have unlocked something worth practising.
  7. Return early. Coming home with a useful haul beats losing time on a preventable recovery.
  8. Look at the map before running. A marker can be nearby in a straight line but awkward to reach.
  9. Explain what you need. “I’m missing this ingredient for this recipe” gets better help than “crafting is broken.”
  10. Leave the next player ready. Replace communal supplies you use and report shortages.

Do not copy a veteran’s loadout blindly

The screenshots were taken with an established character. Their skill levels, armour, spells and supplies are not what you should expect on a new character. Use each image to understand the interface, then choose equipment you can maintain.

When a guide seems wrong

Compare its date and game version with yours. Check the current item tooltip, then recent Jagex notes. Custom-server settings can also explain differences. This guide was reviewed on 9 October 2026; it is a static edition, not an automatically updating wiki.

A useful end-of-session routine

  • Return to a safe location.
  • Repair gear and organise storage.
  • Record the next material or quest stage you want to work on.
  • Let the group know about a new route, hazard or useful discovery.
  • Leave through the game’s normal menu when you finish.
