Workstations & production chains

Build the right station, then understand what goes in and what comes out.

Start with the job

A Crafting Table handles early essentials. Later equipment is split across specialised benches. The Blacksmith’s Bench replaced the older Smithing Anvil; Armour Bench, Fletching Bench and Mystic Forge serve different equipment types.

Use the recipe finder to identify the correct station for an item before spending materials on construction.

Placement and unlocking

StationUseful requirement
FurnaceAcquire copper or tin to unlock. Uses charcoal as fuel; no roof required. It smelts metal, rather than firing clay.
KilnAcquire clay to unlock. Converts wood into charcoal and swamp tar into adhesive. Can operate indoors.
Spinning WheelAcquire flax; roof required. Produces thread from fibre or suitable fur.
TanneryAcquire bone to unlock. No roof required in the newer snapshot. Upgrades allow recipes with more inputs.
LoomAcquire Bronze Bars; roof required. Turns thread into cloth.
Armour / Blacksmith’s / Fletching / Mystic benchesAcquire Bronze Bars to unlock this workstation tier. The Fletching Bench requires shelter.
Jeweler’s BenchAcquire a Silver Bar to unlock.
Cooking RangeCooking level 16 and the relevant campfire/ingredient discovery. Requires a roof.
Rune AltarNo special placement requirement listed. Keep extra essence for runes after paying its build cost.

For benches without a roof rule listed here, follow the current placement prompt. Space, terrain and updates can affect whether a location is valid.

Three useful production chains

  1. Metal gear: logs → Kiln → Charcoal; ore + Charcoal → Furnace → bars; bars and other components → the equipment’s specialised bench.
  2. Clothing: flax or fur → Spinning Wheel → thread; thread → Loom → cloth; cloth and other components → the appropriate equipment bench.
  3. Runes: Rune Essence → Rune Altar → rune batches. Manual attunement gives more Runecrafting experience than leaving production automatic. Mining essence itself no longer grants Runecrafting XP in the newer patch note.

Why is nothing happening?

  • Check input slots, fuel, output space and any required upgrade.
  • Make sure the station has the shelter it needs.
  • Look for the station’s interaction prompt rather than its build menu.
  • Take finished items from output slots; they may not transfer to your bag automatically.
