How to read the calculator

  • A batch is one recipe execution. Some recipes produce more than one item per batch.
  • Inputs are multiplied by your batch count. The planner does not recursively add ingredients for components you might craft separately.
  • Alternative recipes are separate entries. Choose the version matching the materials you have.
  • Costs describe the saved wiki recipe, before any personal bonuses or custom server modifiers. Fuel and workstation construction are separate.
Field note

This is an offline snapshot, not a live connection to the wiki. Check the in-game recipe when materials differ and use the source link on the selected recipe.

Plan the full workshop

View workstation construction costs and production chains. The planner covers the supplied recipe tables across 20 facilities; it is not an exhaustive database of every item in Dragonwilds.
