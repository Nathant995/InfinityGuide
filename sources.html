Wiki links

The RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki is the deeper reference for individual items, quests and encounters. This edition includes attributed recipe data and adapted guide material from the host-supplied saved wiki pages, with links to each source. Direct automated access to wiki pages was restricted during preparation, so the guide does not claim a complete current audit of the wiki.

Screenshots and artwork

Gameplay screenshots were supplied by the DragonInfinity host in RSDW_InfinityGuide.zip. The underlying game visuals belong to their respective owners, including Jagex. Images are used to illustrate the instructions. No external wiki artwork is bundled. The host authorised including the server password and password-entry screenshot for this privately intended guide.

Local preferences and privacy

Search runs in your browser. Bookmarks, reading progress, checklists and theme preference are stored on this device when browser storage is available. They do not sync to your game or another device. This site has no analytics, account system or embedded third-party fonts. External links take you to other sites.

You will be asked before this clears your bookmarks, reading progress and checklists.

Wiki adaptations and recipe data

The expanded quest, skill, lore and workstation references paraphrase the wiki HTML snapshots supplied by the host. Recipe names, quantities and station construction costs are extracted from those snapshots. Their underlying wiki content is credited to the RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki contributors under CC BY-NC-SA 3.0. These adaptations and the recipe dataset are shared under that same licence. Each guide links its topic sources, and each recipe links its source table.

The archive contained saved HTML but not the companion article-image folders. Screenshots shown here are the host-supplied media from the original package. No missing wiki images are referenced.

Reference edition · 12 October 2026

This edition replaces the previous generic topic summaries with extracted recipe quantities, base XP, fishing catches, regional tool requirements and skill unlock tables. 629 recipe variants across 20 facilities are included; 20 rows lack a numeric XP figure and are shown as “Not recorded”. Sources can contain contradictions; disputed woodcutting unlocks and the Blurite tool requirement are explicitly identified.

Live community references used for the XP curve: GEGEBASE Experience and RuneScape Dragonwilds community XP calculator. Current controller reference: Spottis controls, checked against Jagex’s published 1.0 attack changes.
