Combat guide

Learn a repeatable fighting rhythm before worrying about perfect equipment.

Your first safe fight

  1. Choose the ground. Avoid edges, deep water and groups of enemies. Leave yourself somewhere to retreat.
  2. Watch first. Let the enemy begin an attack so you can recognise its wind-up.
  3. Defend. Block or dodge the attack rather than trading hits until someone falls over.
  4. Reply briefly. Attack during the opening, then stop before you exhaust your stamina.
  5. Reset. Reposition and repeat. Heal or leave if the fight is going badly.
Field note

Stamina is your action budget. Spending every last bit on attacks can leave you unable to avoid the next hit. Keeping a little in reserve is often worth more than one extra swing.

Choose a style to learn

StyleWhat it asks of youA sensible first habit
MeleeFight close enough to be hit.Practise defending and short counterattacks.
RangedAim while managing distance and ammunition.Check ammo before leaving and keep a retreat route.
MagicManage your weapon, runes and casting openings.Know what each attack costs before a long fight.

Try the equipment available to you before committing all your resources to one style. Use item descriptions to compare damage, protection and set effects. Better equipment will not remove the need to react.

Power, health and special attacks

Inspect your equipped items and power level in the inventory. If an encounter feels far beyond you, improve your equipment and preparation or return with the team. Do not judge your readiness from a single high skill level.

The 1.0 combat update gives special attacks their own recharging bar. Save these attacks for a useful opening. The same update changes parry behaviour, so old videos may not match what you see. Use your current game’s prompts and tooltips.

Fighting with the group

  • Agree who will engage first and where everyone will stand.
  • Give ranged players a clear view and do not drag enemies into an unprepared teammate.
  • Call out when you need to retreat. A coordinated reset is better than a chain of recovery runs.
  • Check the server’s PvP setting. The supplied browser image shows it enabled; avoid testing attacks beside teammates.
  • Before a major encounter, use the boss preparation checklist.
