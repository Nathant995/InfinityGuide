Dragon Slayer

Follow Cathan’s trail, make the Dragonkin Horn and face Velgar.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 12 OCT 2026
On this page
  • Before you set out
  • Follow the blue flames
  • Make the Dragonkin Horn
  • Survive Velgar

Before you set out

Start with the Wise Old Man in Bramblemead Village after First Steps. Prepare a sheltered, claimed bedroll and a reliable source of food. Deer provide antlers; a ranged weapon makes hunting easier. Work through the Journal’s preparations before pushing into dangerous regions.

Quest preparation

This walkthrough contains story and boss spoilers. Upgrade your equipment as you travel; a quest marker is not a promise that your current gear is strong enough.

Follow the blue flames

  1. Explore the Keep of the Blue Flames and find Cathan’s journal in the chest at the top. Be ready to move out of Velgar’s poison after the encounter.
  2. In Whispering Swamp, enter the blue-flame vault Vertentis Kara. Work through it and speak to Spitfoot at the end. Return to the Wise Old Man.
  3. Continue to Velgar’s Rise in Ghornfell’s Fractured Plains. Cathan’s ghost explains the Dragonkin Horn.

Make the Dragonkin Horn

MaterialWhere and how
1 Dragon ToothUse an Iron Pickaxe on the dragon skull in western Stormtouched Highlands, beyond the Garou skeleton settlement.
1 Bloodwood SapUse an Iron Logging Axe on the Bloodwood Tree at the southern castle in Bloodblight Swamp. Prepare for poison.
5 Bronze BarsSmelt copper and tin in a Furnace. Use the recipe finder to plan your materials.

Craft the horn with the unlocked recipe. Bring food, repaired equipment, healing and antipoison supplies before taking it to the Dragonkin Altar at the top of Velgar’s Rise.

Survive Velgar

  • Watch the ground: poison spray and lingering pools restrict where you can stand. Move to clear ground before attacking.
  • At close range, expect bites, head swings and leg attacks. Dodge the tell, then make a short attack; do not spend all your stamina.
  • At roughly 75%, 50% and 25% health, Velgar takes flight and sends poison across the arena. Stay mobile and watch the next corner for the landing.
  • Keep a clear escape route. If a teammate needs help, deal with the ground hazards before attempting a rescue.

Leave inventory space for the head. After victory, return to the Wise Old Man outside the tower to finish the quest.
