A new adventure in the RuneScape universe

Dragonwilds is a survival crafting game set on Ashenfall, a forgotten continent in RuneScape’s world. You move, aim and fight directly, gather materials and build a home. You do not need to have played RuneScape or Old School RuneScape to start.

Ashenfall is rich in anima, the magical energy that helps explain its extraordinary landscape and powerful skill spells. Ruins and old accounts reveal what happened here. Familiar faces such as the Wise Old Man, Doric and Zanik connect this frontier to the wider RuneScape setting.

What am I trying to achieve?

The original Kuldra Saga builds towards confronting the Dragon Queen, Kuldra. Treat that as the long-term destination. Your first useful victory is much smaller: reach camp, learn how to feed and equip yourself, and return safely from a gathering trip.

HorizonA useful goal
First sessionJoin DragonInfinity, reach camp and claim an available bed.
Next few sessionsUnderstand your equipment, choose a combat style and contribute supplies.
The longer adventureExplore further regions, follow quests and prepare for major encounters with the group.
Your own goalsBuild a home, master skills, collect equipment or become the team’s cook and supplier.
Field note

Progress is a loop: gather useful materials, turn them into supplies, explore a little further, then come home better prepared. You do not need to complete every system at once.

What makes DragonInfinity different?

DragonInfinity is the shared server hosted by S3RVNetwork, part of S3rvStudios. Its custom settings can change how quickly you progress and how dangerous the world feels. This guide teaches the underlying game and uses our own screenshots for the server-specific steps.

The supplied server-browser screenshot shows PvP and crossplay enabled. That is a snapshot, not a live status feed. Check View Details before joining and follow the host’s current rules.

2026 / 2027 roadmap

Jagex’s announced plans, summarised from the 3 October 2026 Summit. These are future plans, not features to look for in your current menus. Dates and scope can change.

DECEMBER 2026 · PLANNED

Luminance

Prayer, elven customisation and building pieces, the Lyre, Songstones and Fairy Ring journeys. A prelude to the next saga.

FIRST HALF OF 2027 · PLANNED

Song of Rebellion

The Blood Crystal Saga opens in Ailddinas, with rebel elves, the Nos Clan and the Hunter skill. Official modding tools, an in-game mod browser, Secure Servers and Leaderboards are also targeted for this window.

LATER CHAPTER · ANNOUNCED

Crown of Night

The story continues into the Nos Clan’s eclipsed lands, with the Light of Seren and legendary equipment. Consult Jagex for the latest release timing.
