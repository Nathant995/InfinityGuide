Black Knight’s Fortress walkthrough

Explore the fortress story without losing your bearings.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 09 OCT 2026
THE PRACTICAL REFERENCE

Requirements, unlocks & recipes

How this works

Black Knight's Fortress is the seventh primary quest and the third in the main storyline of RuneScape: Dragonwilds . In this quest, the Player must delve into an ancient mountainous fortress after defeating the revenant dragon Imaru in order to gain access to the Umbral Sands . Speak to Death in the Forgotten Temple. He will mention about how you did well in Fellhollow, but that there is more for you to do. A colleague of his is waiting for you in the Umbral Sands to help him, but instructs you to speak to Cathan to gather allies and investigate. You can find Cathan east of Fellhollow, near the ominous gatehouse in the south of Silverthorn Keep .
