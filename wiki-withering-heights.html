Withering Heights

Enter Fellhollow, recover three curse fragments and challenge Imaru.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 12 OCT 2026
On this page
  • Enter Fellhollow
  • Grief: the four shrines of Emberwood
  • Rage and Despair
  • Hope’s Fall and Imaru

Enter Fellhollow

  1. After Dragon Slayer, speak to the Wise Old Man near Ashien’s Watch. Follow his dialogue to enter the Forgotten Temple.
  2. Speak to Death and Postie Pete. Crossing wither water puts you into Soulrift, allowing passage through the spectral doorway into the Bleakfields.
  3. Head for the purification fountain marked by a blue beacon and compass indicator. Break the spectral tangles around it, then purify yourself.
  4. Speak to Cathan near the cliff and read the three tablets. Your next goal is three curse fragments.
Field note

Soulrift is a gameplay state, not simply a visual effect. Lingering in it brings increasingly dangerous hostile souls. Learn the way to purification before exploring widely.

Grief: the four shrines of Emberwood

ShrineWhat to do
GuthixAt the western druid circle, defeat the Green Dragon and use the altar.
SaradominNorth-east, find the lone rock in the small lake. Repair the submerged symbol and interact with it.
ArmadylAt the river island east of Guthix, solve the crime dialogue. The supplied walkthrough’s option order is 3 → 2 → 3 → 3; follow the dialogue meaning if options change.
ZamorakEnter the temple through its roof, use the altar and pull the lever to open the exit.

Activating all four removes the barrier to the Fragment of Grief.

Rage and Despair

  • Rage: enter Vekchenven Kara beneath the waterfall at Lake of Lost Souls. Complete the vault and defeat its Zogre to obtain the fragment.
  • Despair: ascend Silverthorn Keep through the undead. Defeat the Green Dragon near the curse seal, then interact with the seal.

Hope’s Fall and Imaru

Bring all three fragments to the seal at Hope’s Fall. The supplied walkthrough recommends Power Level 5, with food and potions prepared. Repair before entering.

  • Imaru changes the fight through Soulrift. Watch the arena state and do not let wandering souls become free healing for the boss.
  • Four purification pools are available around the arena. Use them deliberately while avoiding wither rot.
  • Prioritise positioning and survival when the state changes. Re-establish control before committing to damage.

Keep inventory space for Imaru’s head. After the fight, return to Death to complete the quest.
