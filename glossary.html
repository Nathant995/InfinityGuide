The language of Ashenfall

TermWhat it means
AggroAn enemy’s attention: the player or target it is trying to attack.
AnimaMagical energy important to Ashenfall’s setting.
Biome / regionAn area with its own terrain, resources and hazards.
Buff / debuffA beneficial / harmful effect on your character.
CooldownThe wait before an ability can be used again.
CraftingTurning ingredients into a useful item.
DurabilityHow worn your equipment is; repair it when needed.
EquipPut an item into use, rather than merely carry it.
Farm / grindRepeat an activity to collect materials or gain progress.
HotbarQuick-access slots for items or equipment.
LootItems collected from enemies, containers or the world.
NPCA character controlled by the game, often someone you speak to for a quest.
Power level / tierAn indicator of equipment progression; separate from a skill’s XP level.
PvE / PvPFighting game-controlled enemies / fighting other players.
RecipeThe requirements and ingredients for making something.
RespawnAppear again after death. Check your world’s recovery rules.
RunesConsumable magical items used to power spells.
StaminaThe resource you spend on actions such as movement and fighting.
TelegraphThe visual or sound warning before an enemy attack.
XPExperience points used to advance a skill.

Ask one clear question

It is fine not to know a term. Try “Which station makes this?” or “What does this warning mean?” and show the relevant screen. Most problems become easier when everyone is talking about the same item or objective.
