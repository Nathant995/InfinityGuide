How this works

The armour bench is a crafting station that allows the creation of head armour, body armour, leg armour (all styles: melee, magic, ranged), cape slot equipment (capes + other ). This replaces, in part, the functionality of the smithing anvil , crafting table , and the jeweler's bench .

Use these tables to check the exact requirements in your supplied Dragonwilds wiki snapshot. Search for an item, ingredient, station or level. Recipes may change with game updates.

At a glance

A station is useful only when the recipe is unlocked and you have the correct input materials. Check the Journal for exact ingredients and unlock conditions; recipes may change with updates.

The armour bench is a crafting station that allows the creation of head armour, body armour, leg armour (all styles: melee, magic, ranged), cape slot equipment (capes + other ). This replaces, in part, the functionality of the smithing anvil , crafting table , and the jeweler's bench

Wiki reference

This page is a player-friendly overview based on the supplied RuneScape: Dragonwilds wiki snapshot. Confirm exact requirements against the live game and the full linked article.

What to do

  1. Find or build this station at an accessible crafting area.
  2. Inspect the recipe you want to produce and identify missing materials.
  3. Gather or process its inputs at any prerequisite stations.
  4. Use the station interface to craft your item and collect the output.
  5. Store excess materials in the correctly labelled communal chest.

Practical advice for DragonInfinity

  • Check the server rules before changing shared builds, supplies or the world state.
  • If an activity seems unavailable, check your Journal, recipe unlocks and equipped tool tier.
  • Use the search bar to find related topics and the wiki link below for a more detailed reference.
