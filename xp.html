Where these numbers come from

The numeric table is transcribed from GEGEBASE’s Experience reference, which credits the RuneScape: Dragonwilds Wiki. Milestone totals were cross-checked against the community XP calculator on 12 October 2026. We use the displayed totals, not the conflicting formula prose found on some copies.

The calculator rounds actions up. It assumes each action awards the same XP and does not add food, equipment or potion bonuses. For a recipe that makes several items, enter its XP per batch; for fishing, enter XP per catch.

◇Skill references

Fishing · XP & unlocks

Make a Coarse Net from 2 Coarse Thread at a Crafting Table. Net fish until Fishing 3, then craft an Ash Rod from 1 Ash Log + 1 Coarse Thread. Net and rod spots are separate. Bait reduces junk; pulling against the fish tires it before reeling.

Artisan · XP & unlocks

Process materials you already gather: flax into thread, logs into charcoal, and ore into bars. The recipe database records XP per batch, so compare output quantity before assuming the displayed XP is per item. Sawmill recipes in this snapshot award 0 XP.
