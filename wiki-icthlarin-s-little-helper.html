Icthlarin’s Little Helper

Earn the trust of three Garou clans, recover the lenses and open the Burning Spire.

3 MIN READREVIEWED 12 OCT 2026
On this page

  • Alcarrid: earn your first lens

Arrive in the Umbral Sands

After the Titan, follow Avisk’s objective through the Bastion doorway. At Alcarrid Oasis, meet Shangruuh, Ancamairon and Icthlarin. Three clans hold the lenses needed to repair the mirrors.

Field note

Prepare drinks and food that help against Scorch, and consider travelling after resting for the night. Bring a spade, a Mithril Pickaxe or better, and an Adamant Logging Axe for the relevant gathering tasks.

Alcarrid: earn your first lens

Character / taskWhat to bring or do
Domri · Rogue TraderGather 3 Moonstones from the marked gypsum nodes to the west with a Mithril Pickaxe.
Valas · Mirror, Crack’dProvide 3 Adamant Bars. If smelting them, plan for 9 Adamantite Ore and 9 Coal.
Lagra · Rag and BoneBring 3 raw farm meats from goats or vultures, 3 Kalphite Shell Fragments and 3 Cactus Steaks. Bunny cactus needs an Adamant Logging Axe.

Return to the clan elder after finishing the tasks to receive the lens and progress the quest.

Manafem: recover stories and find Efina

  • Laguna: enter the vault south of the camp at the marked Kalphite nest. Use the Scarab Staff to manage the scarabs. Find three statues: the first right after the staff, another east of the lever, and one on a platform reached with Windstep. Bring Air Runes. The optional Kalphite Queen route is not required for this step.
  • Hedric: investigate the three clues east of camp, including the bodies and scorch marks. Continue east and defeat the Red Dragon for the Radiant Heart. Return to Hedric.
  • Manathep: find Efina by following Dearg Due west of the pyramid in the sandstorm. Give her one Clean Water and return.

Report to elder Dushlanach once the clan tasks are complete.

Uzzer: records, monuments and councillors

  • Lehran: dig up four scrolls at the southern ruins: Schism, Sun Demon, Old Kingdom and Moon Goddess. Some are behind gates; defend against Kot-Haar.
  • Laguna: investigate the statues at four rock spires around the pyramid. Bring a torch and a pickaxe for blocked sandstone caves. The final statue is by the Nightmare Crucible of Humiliation. Return to Laguna.
  • Shangruuh: persuade the three councillors. Alabrais needs the evidence about Fuzan’s control and destruction. Brialka responds to the history of choice and strength through Garou unity. Chirot’s argument concerns the cost of action and what Fuzan takes.
Councillor dialogue hints

Follow the meaning of the choices, since wording can change. For Alabrais: Fuzan seeks control like the Kot-Haar; the evidence points to destruction; control remains constant. For Brialka: each generation chooses; alliances made the Garou stronger; unity helped them survive. For Chirot: action costs money; consider wealth before and after Fuzan’s share; Fuzan steals what Chirot hopes to take.

Return to elder Gaimbin for the clan’s lens.

The mirrors, the trials and Fuzan

  1. Return to Icthlarin with all three lenses. Repair the three mirrors to open the Burning Spire gateway.
  2. Prepare a complete combat loadout before entering the Fight Caves. The Trials of Fuzan contain ten waves; use the breaks to reassess supplies.
  3. Continue to Fuzan’s lair, defeat him and take the head back to complete the quest.
Field note

This is the quest route, not a complete wave-by-wave combat reference. Use the linked wiki pages for encounter detail, and agree on a group plan before entering.
