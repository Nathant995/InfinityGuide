Boss guide

Prepare for the encounter, learn its signals and fight as a team.

Be ready before you trigger it

Bosses are substantial encounters, not simply larger versions of the first creature you fought. A failed attempt can cost time and supplies. Arrange a start time, compare readiness and make sure everyone knows where to regroup.

This is a preparation guide and encounter directory. For exact phases, summons, weaknesses and current item requirements, use the named wiki encounter links below before setting out.

A good first attempt

  1. Spend the opening watching the boss’s movement and attack signals.
  2. Move out of obvious danger before trying to finish a combo.
  3. Use short attacks during recovery windows, then reposition.
  4. Deal with additional enemies when they prevent safe movement.
  5. Use supplies before the situation becomes unrecoverable.
  6. After an attempt, name one thing to improve: positioning, equipment, timing or preparation.
Field note

These are general combat recommendations. They do not replace an encounter’s specific mechanics, and not every boss attack can be handled in the same way.

Avoid a recovery spiral

Do not repeatedly run back alone with no gear or supplies. Pause, ask for help and make a recovery plan. Tell the group where you died and what threat remains there. If the team is not ready, return later rather than spending everyone’s stock on identical attempts.
