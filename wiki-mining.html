How to train it

Mine copper and tin with a Bone Pickaxe; return to smelt both into Bronze Bars. A Mining level does not replace the required pickaxe tier. Keep charcoal production running so the ore does not sit unused.

A tool-tier discrepancy

The saved pickaxe summary includes Blurite under Bronze, while its resource-location table requires Iron. Take an Iron Pickaxe to Takla Kara to satisfy the stricter requirement. The per-level critical bonus also differs from some community copies; use the in-game skill screen for that passive.
