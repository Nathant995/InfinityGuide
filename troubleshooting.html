Help & troubleshooting

Work out what is wrong before you throw away your progress.

2 MIN READREVIEWED 09 OCT 2026
On this page
  • Connection problems
  • Gameplay problems
  • Report a problem clearly
  • Find deeper answers

Connection problems

I cannot find DragonInfinity

Check spelling, filters, crossplay and updates in the world browser. Ask the host whether the server is online. Follow the connection guide to compare your screen with the supplied example.

The password is rejected

Use the latest password from the host. Check capitals and unwanted spaces. Do not post the password or your account details publicly.

I joined, but the camp is missing

Check the world name. You may be in a local world or a similarly named server. Compare your map with the host’s annotated camp route.

Gameplay problems

I cannot mine or chop this resource

Check your equipped tool, its condition and the resource requirement. A tool-tier warning needs a suitable tool; repeated swings will not bypass it.

The recipe is missing or greyed out

Check the correct station, recipe category, unlock and exact ingredients. Review processed materials as well as raw materials. See crafting.

My spell is unlocked but does nothing

Check assignment, runes, cooldown and targeting. Read the spell checklist.

The quest marker is there but nothing happens

Read the stage wording and inspect nearby entrances or levels. Check required items and return dialogue. See quest troubleshooting.

Why am I weaker than the character in the screenshots?

The host used an established character. Skill levels, gear, spells and custom settings can all differ. Start with the first-session guide rather than trying to reproduce the screenshots’ loadout.

Report a problem clearly

Tell the host: the world name, your platform and game version, what you were trying to do, the exact error, and whether it happens again. A screenshot of the problem helps; hide any passwords or private account details.

Field note

Avoid deleting characters or saves as a first fix. Diagnose the issue and ask the host before making changes that could lose progress.

Find deeper answers

Use site search for a short explanation. If you need exact recipe numbers, spawn locations or a full encounter walkthrough, open the relevant official wiki page from the bottom of the guide. Jagex’s help centre and news pages are useful for controls, troubleshooting and changed features.
